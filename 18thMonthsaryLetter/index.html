Chapter one · what eighteen months became

Not a perfect story. Our story.

The thing I love most is that our relationship is made from real days — the soft ones, the heavy ones, the silly ones, and every time we still find our way back to each other.

01 · the beginning

You became my favorite person.

Somewhere between ordinary conversations and small moments, being with you started to feel like home.

02 · your laughter

You make heavy days lighter.

Kana imong tinuod nga katawa remains one of my favorite sounds — the kind that can change the mood of an entire day.

03 · the hard days

Love is also patience.

We have had trials and misunderstandings, but I still want to meet those days with gentleness, patience, and us.

04 · the quiet moments

Peace can look like sitting beside you.

Late-night talks, soft kisses, comfortable silence — the small things are often the memories I keep closest.

05 · the choosing

I still choose us.

Not because everything is easy, but because every version of us has taught me that love is something we keep choosing.

06 · what comes next

There is still so much ahead.

More laughter. More softness. More growing. More memories we have not even met yet.
