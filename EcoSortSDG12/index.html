THE PROBLEM

Why does waste segregation matter?

When different types of waste are mixed together, useful recyclable materials can become harder to recover, while other waste may require different handling. Lack of information can make proper segregation difficult.

Mixed Waste

Putting different waste types in one container can make sorting and recovery more difficult.

E-Waste

Old electronic devices should not simply be treated like ordinary household waste.

Overconsumption

Buying only what we need and choosing reusable items can reduce unnecessary waste.

SORT GUIDE

Where does it belong?

Use this simple guide as a starting point. Follow your local waste-management rules when they differ.

Biodegradable

Food scraps, fruit and vegetable waste, and other biodegradable materials.

Recyclable

Clean paper, cardboard, and suitable recyclable containers.

Residual

Waste that cannot be composted or practically recycled.

Special / E-Waste

Batteries and old electronics need appropriate collection or disposal channels.
